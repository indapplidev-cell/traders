## Exact explanation

The apparent `BEST_NET_PNL > 0` versus `BEST_PF < 1` discrepancy compares two
different configs, not two inconsistent aggregates. The best-Net-PnL config
admits one validation winner (`+1.01784916`) and no loss. The unchanged formula
returns PF `null` when the loss denominator is absent. The PF leader therefore
comes from the subset of accepted numeric configs whose PF is available; it
admits `+1.01784916`, `-0.47794874`, and `-0.58951984`, giving Net PnL
`-0.04961941999999997` and PF `1.01784916 / 1.06746858 =
0.9535167395746674`. Both metrics use validation-only admitted persisted net
realized PnL. No accounting or reporting bug exists.

The persisted value already subtracts entry and exit fees from fill-price gross
PnL. Foundation slippage is embodied in the persisted simulated fill prices;
the sweep does not deduct it again. Spread is not a separate term in the
realized-PnL formula and is present only to the extent already embodied in fill
prices.
