## Production defect reproduction and remediation

The first common 16:30 UTC boundary reproduced a compatibility race absent
from isolated tests. The unchanged deployed legacy 15m image could claim 5m
`WAITING_FOR_REQUIRED_BOUNDARY` rows. Seven of ten 5m run rows were finished
by that legacy runner and joined to 15m-labeled results. No command, order,
fill, position, executable flag, approval or LIVE action was created. The new
5m process was stopped immediately; 15m continued uninterrupted.

The final remediation is confined to the non-default profile. Retryable
freshness is checked before authoritative 5m run reservation. At 16:40:18 UTC
the not-yet-ready boundary had zero runs and zero WAITING rows. Once ready it
completed exact10 as 5m. Boundaries 16:40, 16:45 and 16:50 each completed
10/10 with nested timeframe `5m`, the authoritative 5m parameter identity in
every stage, and zero trading mutations. At 16:45, unchanged 15m and protected
5m both completed exact10 with distinct run IDs and profile IDs.

The seven reproduced shadow-only misclaims remain immutable audit history;
they were not deleted, rewritten or hidden. At 16:53 UTC there were 90 5m run
rows and 90 joined result rows: 83 5m-labeled plus the seven reproduced legacy
misclaims. All 30 post-fix rows were 5m-labeled and stage-consistent.
