The incident scope was determined by testing credential candidates internally,
not by printing or fingerprinting them. Two active credentials present in the
offending environment inspection were rotated. Stale `traders_ml` environment
values were already invalid and the active shared-file credential had not been
present in Docker environment output, so that unrelated credential was not
rotated again.

Post-rotation validation used fresh natural 15m and 5m boundaries. Both were
exact 10/10 with zero error or safety rows, while commands, orders, fills, and
positions remained 0/0/0/0. The pending Readonly i18n feature deployment from
the interrupted Scalping task was deliberately not mixed into this security
remediation.
