Closed-candle filtering, profile-aware persistence/dedupe and the shadow-to-PAPER
firewall are present. The blocker is earlier than production activation:
`TradeSearchProfile` ATR, impulse, structure, confirmation, volume and regime
fields are referenced only by the registry and its tests. `PipelineRunner`
passes generic required history to `OnlineAnalysisConfig`, while setup, strategy
and risk use generic defaults. Effective 5m-specific semantics are therefore
not proven.

The runtime also has no profile singleton lease, PostgreSQL advisory lock or
equivalent owner guard. Per-window unique reservation prevents duplicate rows
but does not prevent two independently named 5m workers from running.

The current 15m image does not contain `trade_profile.py`; other inspected
orchestrator source hashes also differ from the accepted tree. A separate image
would be required, but none was built after the mandatory source gates failed.
No production start, cursor initialization, schema change, Control POST, grant,
PAPER mutation, service restart or image replacement occurred.
